About NoorTools

Our mission and values

Our Mission

NoorTools is dedicated to providing clean, simple, and free Islamic tools and resources for Muslims worldwide. We believe that access to Quranic knowledge, Islamic calculators, and daily Islamic utilities should be available to everyone without barriers, ads, or data collection.

What We Offer

  • 📖 Quran Interface - Read the Quran with authentic translations and Tafsir
  • 🔧 Islamic Tools - Zakat calculator, age calculator, date converter, and more
  • 🤲 Duas - Collection of authentic Islamic duas for daily life
  • ⚡ Fast & Efficient - Optimized for speed with no unnecessary scripts
  • 📱 Mobile Friendly - Works perfectly on all devices
  • 🔒 Private & Safe - No tracking, no ads, no data collection

Our Commitment

Authenticity: We do not fabricate, generate, or invent Islamic information. All religious content is sourced from verified, authentic Islamic sources. When connected to data, we will use only verified Quranic text, authentic Hadith, and scholarly Tafsir.

Simplicity: We keep the code simple, clean, and easy to maintain. No unnecessary frameworks, no bloated dependencies, just vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Privacy: We respect your privacy completely. There are no tracking scripts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party services. Your data is yours alone.

Technology

NoorTools is built with modern web standards using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The entire website is designed to work offline and load instantly. We prioritize user experience and performance over unnecessary complexity.

Important Note

Religious Guidance: NoorTools provides tools and resources, but should not replace consultation with qualified Islamic scholars for important religious matters. Always verify Islamic information with reliable sources and qualified experts before making religious decisions.

Contact & Feedback

We welcome feedback and suggestions for improving NoorTools. Please visit our GitHub repository to report issues or contribute to the project.
