Privacy Policy

How we protect your privacy

✅ Your Privacy is Guaranteed: NoorTools collects NO personal data, uses NO tracking, displays NO ads, and has NO third-party services.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

NoorTools does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • ✓ No user accounts or login information
  • ✓ No browsing history or activity logs
  • ✓ No IP addresses or location tracking
  • ✓ No cookies or local storage (except browser cache)
  • ✓ No form submissions to external servers
  • ✓ No analytics or tracking scripts

2. How Your Data Stays Private

All calculations and interactions happen entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers:

  • 🔒 Local Processing: Zakat, age, and percentage calculations are processed locally in your browser
  • 🔒 No Transmission: Your input data is never sent to any server
  • 🔒 No Third Parties: We don't use any third-party services, CDNs for tracking, or external libraries that collect data
  • 🔒 No Ads: No advertisements, affiliate links, or sponsored content

3. Cookies & Browser Storage

NoorTools does not create cookies or use browser storage to track you. The only data stored locally is your browser's cache, which is automatically managed by your browser and does not contain any personal information.

4. External Links

If NoorTools includes links to external websites, we are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review the privacy policy of any external site before sharing your information with them.

5. Open Source & Transparency

NoorTools is open source and publicly available on GitHub. You can inspect our entire codebase to verify that no tracking, data collection, or malicious code exists. We believe in complete transparency.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. We recommend checking this page regularly to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about our privacy practices, you can:

  • • Visit our GitHub repository and open an issue
  • • Check the About page for contact information

Last Updated

This Privacy Policy is effective as of September 2024.
